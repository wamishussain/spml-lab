# Abstract

We derive an exact finite-sample decomposition of ridge-regression risk 
for structured covariance models and use it to study the spectral 
susceptibility near the interpolation threshold. Numerical experiments 
reveal a finite-λ crossover from ridge-dominated behavior to a 
finite-size regime associated with the lower spectral edge. While 
asymptotic deterministic-equivalent theory describes the large-system 
limit, the observed crossover and its dependence on covariance structure 
highlight finite-size phenomena that are not captured by leading-order 
asymptotics.
